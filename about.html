Our Perspective

Why We Believe in Doing Solar the Right Way

Solar Is a Long-Term Decision

A rooftop system is usually designed to operate for 25 years or more. That makes the quality of design, equipment and installation far more important than the price on a single day. We would rather explain the trade-offs honestly than sell you a bigger system that your roof or your bills do not need.

What We Check Before Every Installation

  • Roof structure and waterproofing, so mounting does not damage the building
  • Shade from trees, water tanks, and neighbouring buildings at different times of day
  • Load pattern and whether appliances can run during daylight hours
  • Cable routing, earthing resistance, and distance from the inverter to the main board
  • Documentation needed for net-metering and any applicable subsidy

Warranties: What to Ask For

When you compare quotations, look at three types of warranty separately. The panel warranty usually covers output over many years. The inverter warranty covers hardware faults for a set period. The installation workmanship warranty covers our work on mounting, wiring and commissioning. Ask us to put the exact terms in writing before you sign.

Common Myths About Solar

Myth: Solar panels stop working when it rains. Fact: They produce less power in cloudy weather but still generate electricity.

Myth: Every home needs a battery. Fact: A grid-tied system with net-metering often works well without one.

Myth: Solar needs constant attention. Fact: Periodic cleaning and checks are usually enough.
