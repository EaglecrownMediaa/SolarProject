In Detail

How Each Service Works

Residential Rooftop Solar Installation

For a home, we begin with a site survey: roof type, orientation, shade and the path for cables to the main board. We then propose a system size based on your bills and daytime usage. Installation includes mounting structures designed for the roof, panel installation, DC and AC wiring, earthing, protection devices, and commissioning. After switch-on, we verify generation and explain the monitoring and safety features.

Commercial and Industrial Solar

Shops, schools, workshops and factories often run heavier daytime loads, which can make solar economics attractive. For these projects we study load profiles over days or weeks, consider existing transformer and panel capacity, and plan phased expansion where needed. Commercial projects also require careful documentation and coordination with the electricity distribution utility.

Inverter Sales and Setup

The right inverter depends on whether you need bill savings, battery backup, or off-grid operation. We check input voltage range, battery voltage and chemistry, output capacity, and expansion options before recommending a model. Setup includes correct configuration for your mode, cable sizing, and testing under real load.

Maintenance and Repair

A healthy solar system is one that is checked regularly. Our maintenance visits typically cover panel cleaning guidance, inspection of connectors and cables for heat or corrosion, earthing checks, inverter error log review, and generation comparison against expected output. If a fault appears, we diagnose it and recommend the fix with a clear cost estimate.

Electrical Engineering Works

Good electrical work is the foundation of a safe installation. We handle earthing pits and resistance testing, lightning and surge protection, DC and AC protection devices, and neat cable management. Work is done according to standard safety practice so the system protects both equipment and people.

Solar Water Pumps

Solar pumps reduce dependence on grid or diesel power for irrigation, gardens, livestock and water storage. Selection depends on the required head (lift height), flow rate, water source depth and daily water need. We size the pump, controller and panels together so the system delivers water reliably through the day.

Care Guide

Simple Maintenance Checklist

Keep this list handy. Most solar issues are easy to prevent with a little regular attention.

Monthly

  • Check the inverter display for error codes
  • Note the daily generation to spot unusual drops
  • Look for visible shade from new growth or structures

Quarterly

  • Clean panels with water and a soft cloth or brush
  • Check cable ties and connectors for looseness
  • Confirm inverter fans and vents are clear

Yearly

  • Full system inspection by a trained technician
  • Earthing resistance check
  • Review performance against the original design
Service FAQ

Questions About Our Services

What is included in a complete installation?

Mounting structure, panels, inverter, DC and AC cabling, protection devices, earthing, commissioning and handover. We confirm the exact scope in the quotation.

Can you help with the net-metering application?

Yes. We prepare the required documents and guide you through the process with the local utility.

How often should a solar system be serviced?

A basic cleaning and visual check every few months and a full inspection once a year is a good routine for most systems.

Do you install on flat concrete roofs and tin sheds?

Yes, subject to a site inspection. Flat concrete roofs use tilted mounting structures, while tin sheds need structural checks before mounting.

What does a commercial project usually need from me?

Recent electricity bills, load details, roof access, and information about existing electrical connections. We will list everything during the first discussion.

Do you offer emergency support?

Please call our office during working hours for urgent issues. We will advise on immediate safety steps and schedule the earliest possible visit.
