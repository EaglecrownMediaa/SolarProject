What information should I keep ready?

Before We Visit

How to Prepare for Your Free Site Visit

A little preparation helps us give you an accurate proposal on the first visit.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Last 2-3 electricity bills
  • Consumer or service number
  • Property ownership or occupancy details

Roof Information

  • Roof type: concrete, tin sheet, tiled or terrace
  • Approximate open area available for panels
  • Any water tanks, trees or buildings nearby

Your Usage

  • List of major appliances and their use hours
  • Plans to add appliances in the future
  • Interest in battery backup, if any
What to Expect

What Happens During the Visit

Our engineer walks the roof and notes the orientation, shade pattern and structural considerations. We check the position of the main electrical board and the cable route from the roof to the inverter location. We also review your bills to understand your daytime and night-time consumption.

After the visit, you receive a written proposal with system size, equipment list, estimated generation, and a line-by-line quotation. There is no pressure to decide on the spot.

Contact FAQ

Everything Before You Reach Out

Do I have to buy after the site visit?

No. The site visit and proposal are free, and you are under no obligation to go ahead.

Can I get a quote without a site visit?

We can give an indicative estimate based on your bill, but an accurate quote needs a site visit to check the roof and wiring path.

Do you serve areas outside Madurai city?

Please share your location when you contact us. We will confirm whether we can serve your area and any site visit charges that may apply.

Can I send my details by WhatsApp instead of the form?

Yes. Message us with your name, area, monthly bill, and a photo of your roof if you have one.

How will my personal details be used?

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry and to arrange the site visit. We do not share them with third parties for marketing.
